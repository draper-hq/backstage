## Design Context

### Users
Consultants, founders and sales teams presenting a client deck live. They need to move through the main story, answer objections from a prepared appendix, keep time and read private prompts without exposing meeting notes on the shared screen.

### Brand Personality
Decisive, editorial and operational. It should feel like a live broadcast rundown prepared by a sharp producer: composed under pressure, legible at a glance and never decorative for decoration's sake.

### Aesthetic Direction
Broadcast modernism in two modes: an off-white public deck with hard editorial composition, and a low-light backstage console for the presenter. The visual reference is a television gallery crossed with a printed run-of-show—not a SaaS dashboard. Avoid Draper's language, generic rounded cards, glowing tech aesthetics, decorative eyebrows and ornamental data widgets.

### Design Principles
- The audience sees the story; the presenter sees the decision support.
- Every label must orient or trigger an action. Remove decorative pre-headings.
- Use lines, sequence and typographic contrast instead of cards and shadows.
- Reserve cobalt and tally red for live state and navigation.
- Make the appendix feel like a routing board: question in, answer out, return to the main conversation.
